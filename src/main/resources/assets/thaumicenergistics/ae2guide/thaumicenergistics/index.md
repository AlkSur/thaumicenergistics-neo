# Thaumic Energistics

Thaumic Energistics connects Thaumcraft to your ME network: essentia is stored on cells like any other resource,
Thaumcraft tubes and infusion altars draw straight from the network, and arcane crafting can be automated.

Everything here is unlocked through the **Thaumic Energistics** tab of the Thaumonomicon; the pages below are a
quick reference, not a replacement for the research.

- [Essentia storage](essentia_storage.md): digitized essentia, cells and the cell workbench
- [Moving essentia](essentia_io.md): access cards, the Essentia Provider, wireless receivers and power from essentia
- [Arcane crafting](arcane_crafting.md): the arcane terminals, vis relays and the Arcane Assembler
- [Neo ECO AE](neoecoae.md): what changes when Neo ECO AE Extension is installed

## Getting started

1. Research **Basic Energistics**, then grow <ItemLink id="thaumicenergistics:mystic_crystal" /> and press it into
   <ItemLink id="thaumicenergistics:mystic_processor" />. Most devices need this processor.
2. Research **Digitized Essentia** to craft the <ItemLink id="thaumicenergistics:diffusion_core" /> and
   <ItemLink id="thaumicenergistics:coalescence_core" />, the base of every essentia device.
3. Make an essentia cell and an <ItemLink id="thaumicenergistics:essentia_provider" />: from then on your alchemy
   setup can feed and drain the network.
